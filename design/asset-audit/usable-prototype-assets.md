# Usable prototype asset list

Prototype assets that could be used **temporarily** once the owner approves. Nothing here has been copied into `public/` or the manifest: the `proposed_target_path` is a _new_ file next to the existing placeholder SVGs, which stay in place until a switch is approved. Every asset is prototype artwork (generated concept-derived cut-outs and crops), not production art. The direction check compares each one with the approved Parrot Island reference (see `visual-direction.md`).

Status key: **USABLE_TEMP** = usable as-is for layout and playtesting. **USABLE_TEMP_AFTER_CLEANUP** = usable after a small mechanical fix (speck removal, edge crop), listed in the notes.

## What each id would get, in plain terms

- **Ships (5):** a usable tier set for the shop, inventory and crew camp: `ship.starter`, `ship.explorer`, `ship.voyager`, `ship.captain`, `ship.legendary` (four of those five ids have no manifest entry today). Two need speck removal. Whole-ship icons only; no anchors.
- **World map base:** the ocean SVG only. Island markers and labels must be separate layers.
- **Home Port:** one scene candidate (`scene.home-port`) and six location crops that have no manifest ids yet. Darker than the approved direction.
- **Characters:** portraits only, not sprites. The Pip portrait **does not match** the approved Pip (no pirate hat, different design) and should be treated as a stand-in at best.
- **Island markers:** all six canonical bases (`Chore_Quests_Pirate_Island_Assets_v3/assets/islands/base/`) after speck cleanup; copies of two of them elsewhere in the packs are duplicates. The **Parrot Island token is off-direction** (a rock with a parrot head, not the approved sandy island).

## What is not here

No clean scene exists for Parrot Island, Shipwreck Cove, Skull Reef, Whispering Jungle or Treasure Island; there are no item icons, cosmetics, badges, branding, puzzle art or audio. See `missing-production-assets.md`.
